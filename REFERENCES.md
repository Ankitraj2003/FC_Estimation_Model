# Core Peer-Reviewed References

1. **Bean, G., Huffaker, R.G., & Migliaccio, K.W. (2018).** *Estimating Field Capacity from Volumetric Soil Water Content Time Series Using Automated Processing Algorithms.* Vadose Zone Journal, 17(1), 180073.  
   DOI: [10.2136/vzj2018.04.0073](https://doi.org/10.2136/vzj2018.04.0073)  
   *Role:* Primary literature foundation for automated soil-water cycle screening, wetting thresholds (0.006 m3/m3), nocturnal drainage recession, SWDP-R inverse-time regression, R-FP, and SWDP-K concepts.

2. **Fazackerley, S., & Lawrence, R. (2012).** *Automatic In Situ Determination of Field Capacity Using Soil Moisture Sensors.* Irrigation and Drainage, 61(3), 416–424.  
   DOI: [10.1002/ird.646](https://doi.org/10.1002/ird.646)  
   *Role:* In-situ drainage-curve modeling and sensor-based field capacity determination as an alternative to fixed 2-day waiting conventions.

3. **Savitzky, A., & Golay, M.J.E. (1964).** *Smoothing and Differentiation of Data by Simplified Least Squares Procedures.* Analytical Chemistry, 36(8), 1627–1639.  
   DOI: [10.1021/ac60214a047](https://doi.org/10.1021/ac60214a047)  
   *Role:* Mathematical foundation for local polynomial smoothing and continuous first/second derivative estimation within continuous data segments.

4. **Lin, J., Keogh, E., Wei, L., & Lonardi, S. (2007).** *Experiencing SAX: a novel symbolic representation of time series.* Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery, 15(2), 107–144.  
   DOI: [10.1007/s10618-007-0064-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10618-007-0064-z)  
   *Role:* Methodological basis for Piecewise Aggregate Approximation (PAA) and Gaussian equiprobable breakpoint discretization into symbolic representations.

5. **Roberts, D.R., et al. (2017).** *Cross-validation strategies for data with temporal, spatial or hierarchical structure.* Ecography, 40(8), 913–929.  
   DOI: [10.1111/ecog.02881](https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.02881)  
   *Role:* Scientific justification for structured event-blocked cross-validation and prohibition of random observation splitting in structured environmental data.

6. **USDA-NRCS.** *Soil Quality Indicators: Available Water Capacity.* Natural Resources Conservation Service.  
   *Role:* Conventional benchmark describing practical drainage periods (approximately 1 to 2 days) and acknowledging soil textural dependence of field capacity.

7. **Jiang, X., et al. (2025).** *A novel hybrid framework for combining process-based models with machine learning for streamflow prediction.* Advances in Water Resources, 206, 105177.  
   DOI: [10.1016/j.advwatres.2025.105177](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.advwatres.2025.105177)  
   *Role:* Methodological motivation for process-guided residual error correction combining physical baselines with machine learning.

8. **Zhou, J., et al. (2025).** *Using physical method, machine learning and hybrid method to model soil water movement.* Journal of Hydrology, 652, 132639.  
   DOI: [10.1016/j.jhydrol.2024.132639](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2024.132639)  
   *Role:* Comparative foundation for physical, empirical ML, and hybrid formulations in soil water dynamics.

9. **Li, Y., et al. (2026).** *A PSO-CatBoost algorithm based on soil sensor data: A novel in-situ intelligent method for estimating field capacity.* Agricultural Communications, 4(1), 100156.  
   DOI: [10.1016/j.agrcom.2026.100156](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agrcom.2026.100156)  
   *Role:* Recent literature precedent utilizing CatBoost for in-situ field capacity estimation from soil moisture sensor records.

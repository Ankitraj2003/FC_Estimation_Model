# Research references used in Phase 2.2

1. **Bean, G., Huffaker, R.G., & Migliaccio, K.W. (2018).** *Estimating Field Capacity from Volumetric Soil Water Content Time Series Using Automated Processing Algorithms.* Vadose Zone Journal. DOI: 10.2136/vzj2018.04.0073.
   - Used for: valid soil-water cycle logic, 0.006 m3/m3 wetting response, overnight no-input criterion, steady drying, and soil-water-dynamics FC estimation.
   - https://acsess.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2136/vzj2018.04.0073

2. **Fazackerley, S. & Lawrence, R. (2012).** *Automatic In Situ Determination of Field Capacity Using Soil Moisture Sensors.* Irrigation and Drainage 61:416–424. DOI: 10.1002/ird.646.
   - Used for: drainage-based in-situ FC concept and nonlinear soil-moisture recession modelling.
   - https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ird.646

3. **USDA-NRCS.** *Soil Quality Indicators – Available Water Capacity* and irrigation soil-water guidance.
   - Used for: drainage-based definition of FC and recognition that texture/structure affect drainage time and water retention.

4. **Wang et al. (2026).** *A PSO-CatBoost Algorithm Based on Soil Sensor Data: A Novel In-Situ Intelligent Method for Estimating Field Capacity.* Agricultural Communications. DOI: 10.1016/j.agrcom.2026.100156.
   - Used only as supporting evidence for CatBoost as a candidate sensor-based FC model. PSO optimization was not claimed because the present dataset is too small for defensible large-scale hyperparameter optimization.
   - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949798126000360
